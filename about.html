About MCQify

Built by a Senior, Designed for Class 11 Juniors

MCQify is a dedicated educational platform created specifically to help Class 11 Federal Board (FBISE) students master their subjects. Having been through the same board syllabus, this platform was built by a senior student to bridge the gap between textbook reading and effective exam practice.

With the updated FBISE exam pattern focusing heavily on objective-based questions, MCQify gives you a clean, distraction-free environment to practice chapter-wise MCQs, test your concepts, and prepare smarter for Physics, Mathematics, and Computer Science.

Developer Information

This platform is 100% independently conceptualized, designed, and developed purely through the personal hard work and coding efforts of Javeria Ikram (Class 12) to support fellow Class 11 students in achieving academic excellence.

Note: MCQify is an independent educational platform created for Class 11 FBISE students. While we try our best to ensure accuracy, please cross-verify with your NBF textbooks. If you find any errors, feel free to note them!
